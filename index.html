OrderRelay MM

Private application for quantitative trading research, portfolio and risk management across the owner's own trading accounts, with integration of the cTrader Open API.

Private use only

OrderRelay MM is a private, single-user tool. It is used exclusively by its owner, Martin Mainz, on the owner's own trading accounts.

  • It is not a commercial product and is not sold, offered or provided to anyone else.
  • There is no public sign-up, no download and no customer access.
  • Only the owner's own cTrader accounts are connected – never third-party accounts.
  • Orders are placed only on the owner's own accounts and only on the owner's own instructions.

About OrderRelay MM

OrderRelay MM is a private application that distributes the owner's own trading signals across the owner's own trading accounts. It calculates position sizes and risk per account and keeps an overview of open positions and orders.

The application runs locally on the owner's computer and is not distributed to third parties.

cTrader Integration

OrderRelay MM uses the official cTrader Open API to connect cTrader accounts that the account owner has explicitly authorised through the official cTrader OAuth process.

  • Account information, symbols, prices, positions and orders are read to calculate risk and to reconcile the portfolio.
  • Orders are only placed if the owner has granted trading permission and has enabled execution for that account. Execution can be switched off at any time.
  • OrderRelay MM is not a broker, holds no client funds and gives no investment advice.

Privacy

Retrieved account data is used only for the functions of the application requested by the account owner and stays on the owner's computer.

Credentials, API keys, client secrets and authorisation tokens are never published on this website.

Access to a cTrader account can be revoked at any time in the cTrader account settings.
